## 02. Observed failure and confirmed mechanism

## 03. Impact and measurement boundary

The attributable interruption lasted 26 minutes, from 2025-08-19T09:00:00Z to 2025-08-19T09:26:00Z. The service recorded 6,500 failed or rejected attempts. This is an attempt count, not unique payments or customers: one business instruction can generate several attempts. Unique customer impact and exact currency loss were not recorded. No assumed transaction value has been attached to the attempt count.

## 04. Response and recovery

The handover retained observation times and the original service and operation references. Responders separated uncertain outcomes from confirmed failures so that recovery did not silently convert pending work into successful or lost payments.

## 07. Operational context

Merchant payment processing crosses an outbound provider boundary. Waiting work, active connections, and client-visible responses describe different parts of that journey. A useful investigation preserves those distinctions and relates observations to the particular configuration and procedure in force.
