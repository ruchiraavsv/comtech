## 02. Observed failure and confirmed mechanism

A stalled SettlementHub consumer partition left an increasing age of unprocessed events while other partitions continued. Aggregate process availability obscured the delayed subset.

<a id="section-03"></a>

## 03. Impact and measurement boundary

The attributable interruption lasted 27 minutes, from 2026-03-02T09:00:00Z to 2026-03-02T09:27:00Z. The service recorded 8,100 failed or rejected attempts. This is an attempt count, not unique payments or customers: one business instruction can generate several attempts. Unique customer impact and exact currency loss were not recorded. No assumed transaction value has been attached to the attempt count.

<a id="section-04"></a>

## 04. Response and recovery

The responder examined partition progress, restored the stalled consumer path, and verified reconciliation of outstanding events before advancing the reporting cutoff. The handover retained observation times and the original service and operation references. Responders separated uncertain outcomes from confirmed failures so that recovery did not silently convert pending work into successful or lost payments.

## 05. Change assessment and follow-up

The investigation does not classify this event as change-related. That classification does not imply that the service had never been deployed or modified; it describes the evidence for this particular interruption. Recovery observations, the initiating mechanism, and longer-term remediation are retained as separate parts of the record. A proposed follow-up remains a proposal until its own decision and implementation evidence exists.

<a id="section-06"></a>

## 06. Interpretation limits

A running consumer group alone would have overstated recovery. The report concerns event backlog, not a confirmed financial loss from delayed reporting.

<a id="section-07"></a>

## 07. Operational context

Settlement and reporting follow payment acceptance through asynchronous processing stages. Each stage can complete at a different time, and a report can be accurate for its stated cutoff while omitting more recent work. Investigation therefore follows source identifiers through event consumption, reconciliation, and publication. Delay, mismatch, and accounting correction are separate observations. Combining them under a single label without tracing their respective records can conceal both the actual processing bottleneck and the required control response.
