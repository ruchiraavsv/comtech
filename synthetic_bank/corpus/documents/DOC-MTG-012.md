## 01. Discussion and disposition

The audit closure committee accepts the independent verification of two completed privileged-access review cycles. Acceptance follows the verification record, not merely the implementation owner's statement that the workflow was delivered.

<a id="section-02"></a>

## 02. Supporting detail

The committee retains the original finding, revised policy, workflow release, and verification references together. Ongoing monthly reviews remain required after closure, and any new gap must be assessed on its own evidence.

<a id="section-03"></a>

## 03. Scope and interpretation

This acceptance closes the reviewed remediation action. It is not a waiver of future reviews or permission to disclose restricted implementation evidence to a broader audience.

<a id="section-04"></a>

## 04. Business and service context

Access-control assurance depends on a chain from requirement to implementation to demonstrated operation. A plan shows intended work; a release shows a mechanism was installed; review evidence shows what actually happened during an operating period. Each record has a different author and purpose. An auditor needs the relevant population and disposition history to assess coverage, while the action owner needs unresolved items to remain visible until their completion can be evidenced.

<a id="section-05"></a>

## 05. Record and follow-up

These minutes preserve the distinction between discussion, accepted decisions, and work assigned for later completion. An action item remains open until the responsible record provides evidence of delivery or an authorized decision changes its disposition. Readers should use the subsequent policy, architecture decision, release, or verification record for the authority specific to that stage. The meeting record supplies the rationale and unresolved questions known at the time.

<a id="section-06"></a>

## 06. Application example

The closure package should let a later reviewer navigate from the original missing evidence to the revised requirement, delivered workflow, and completed verification. If one link is absent, the committee's acceptance statement alone may not explain the basis for closure. Preserve those references with their dates. Continued monthly operation belongs to subsequent review records; closure of this action does not mean that future missed reviews can be treated as already examined or implicitly accepted.
